## Retirement

Q: How can retirees avoid paying ordinary taxes when they must take annual RMD from their traditional IRAs, beginning at age 73?
A: Retires can use their traditional IRAs to make tax-efficient giving and satisfy their required minimum distributions without increasing their adjusted gross income. The funds must come directly from the IRA to the charity.
