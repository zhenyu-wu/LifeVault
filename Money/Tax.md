#### Form 8606

It is used for non-deductible IRA (after-tax) contributions to track IRA basis. 

- You must file it **every year** you make a nondeductible contribution, even if you had no distributions.
- There is a **$50 penalty** for failing to file it when required.
- Keep copies of all past Form 8606s — your cumulative basis carries forward year to year.
- It is **not** the same as reporting deductible IRA contributions (those are handled directly on Schedule 1 of the 1040).
