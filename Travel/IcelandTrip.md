#### 8/18

We began our trip on 8/17 evening from EWR and landed on KEF on 8/18 morning. After a night of flight, I felt a bit dizzy heading out of the airport. After getting our rental car, we went straight to Reykjavik's downtown old harbor area.  We strolled along the bank, looking over the islands across and the sea. We visited a couple of gift shops and had an expensive lunch of Japanese noodles, before heading to the famous Hallgrimskirkja church. Its structure is iconic, with big columns on the two sides of its façade. It is said to be inspired by the basalt rocks common in the country.  Ryan and Melody took the elevator to the top platform to oversee the entire city.

After the church, we drove to our 2nd stop: the Perlan museum. There we took two tours. In the 1st tour, we go through an ice cave not that big. In the 2nd tour, we were shown a geographic introduction film followed by another 3D-max, which takes you deep into a volcano. The feeling was really and astonishing.

Leaving the museum, we made a stop to Costco to have some grocery shopping and diner, before going to our Airbnb.
